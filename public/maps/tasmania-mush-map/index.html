When to go (fruiting conditions)

Season (Tasmania)

  • Onset: Late March to mid April
  • Peak: April, May, June
  • Tail: Extends into July or August

Triggers to watch for

  • Several days of rain (>10mm/day)
  • Night temperatures consistently below 8°C
  • Morning dew or mist on south facing slopes

Disturbance age guide

  • 4 to 8 yr post harvest: Prime, peak diversity
  • 9 to 12 yr: Still excellent, late peak
  • 13 to 18 yr: Declining but productive
  • 2023 (3 yr): Too fresh, fungi just colonising
